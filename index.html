FAQ

Questions, answered.

Where do the products come from?

Real, live marketplace listings collected by our own scraper. Every card links directly to the supplier's product page. No mock data.

What exactly are the AI picks?

Each cycle, our AI reviews the product pool and shortlists the most promising ideas, with a written reason for each: why it stands out, which sellers it fits, and the margin angle.

Will I see the same products every day?

No. Each company's feed only shows products it hasn't seen before, so your daily batch is always fresh ideas.

How does signup work?

Create an account with a username, email and password. We email you a confirmation link, then you log in with your email or username. Passwords are stored as secure hashes, never in plain text.

How do the weekly plans work?

Plans set how many companies you can track and how many fresh products each company gets per day. Your plan is activated after signup.

Company

Skunara is built by Verse DKH, an independent software studio. We started Skunara because product research was the most tedious part of running an online store: hours of scrolling marketplaces, guessing at demand, and spreadsheets that went stale in a week.

So we automated the tedious part. The scraper watches the marketplaces, the AI does the shortlisting, and you get the fun part: picking winners.
